Journey prototype Built from the user journey in docs/design. Scrolling the site lights its step in the chart; clicking a step moves the site there.
About this build

The record behind the site is the archetype build of 2 September 2026. It covers ten humanities fields of study and fifteen kinds of work, with years counted from the first job. Anything named on the site was done by ten or more people.

The lines on the ten-year strip are simulated from the record's measured year-to-year moves, so no line is a real person. Counts and shares in the text and tables are real. The numbers in the margin are the journey's step numbers; they are annotation for this prototype and not part of the site.

Revised 15 September 2026. The site column, the strip and the sentence control are new; the journey chart is unchanged from the 14 September revision.
