Career archetypes, interactive demo

Where does your work sit among two million careers?

Each dot is one of the 2,000 most common job titles in the LinkedIn snapshot, placed near titles whose holders describe similar work, sized by how many people hold it, and colored by the family a model assigned it from the NHA framework. Describe your own work and the page places you among your nearest titles and shows which families the evidence points to.
