## Missed by all three

M1. Thirty-eight percent of the "humanities L1" population under the any-degree definition never took a humanities bachelor's. hum_l1_any is true for 293,808 persons; hum_l1_bachelor_pooled_any is true for 181,767; the 112,041 in between (38.1 percent) hold their humanities credential at another level: associate 46,847, master's 16,823, high school 10,955, level 2 9,115, doctorate 3,410, level unknown 24,158. The cohorts and archetypes modules define humanities on the any-degree flags (build_panel.py:82-91; archetypes/common.py:164-183), so every entry-axis number in play (A5, A7, A8, B2, B5) describes a population of which more than a third did not study humanities as undergraduates. For a site whose premise is "people with your degree", the bachelor-level flag is the only defensible membership rule, and it shrinks the entry-axis population accordingly. VERIFIED:

```
SELECT count(*) FILTER (WHERE hum_l1_any), count(*) FILTER (WHERE hum_l1_any AND NOT hum_l1_bachelor_pooled_any) FROM 'normalized/education_person.parquet'  -- 293808 | 112041

SELECT lvl, count(*) FROM r GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 1  -- 1:10955 | 2:9115 | 3:46847 | 5:733 | 6:16823 | 7:3410 | NULL:24158
```

M2. Early-career observation decays with cohort age, so "ways in" and corridors are drawn mostly from recent graduates.
